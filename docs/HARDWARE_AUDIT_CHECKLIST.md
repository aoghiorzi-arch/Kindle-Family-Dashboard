# Read-only Kindle feasibility audit (P1) — checklist

**Status: NOT PERFORMED. No evidence has been collected from any physical device.**

This is a record template for a later, supervised audit. It authorises *observation only*. It does not authorise rooting, jailbreaking, flashing, firmware updates, installing or running anything on the device, connecting it to a network it is not already on, or leaving an unassessed battery charging unattended. Each item needs the owner's presence and understanding before it is done.

## Rules for the audit
1. Power the device off and inspect the battery area and casing before any charging.
2. Use the device's own menus only. Do not use USB debugging, developer modes, unknown scripts or third-party tools.
3. Do not connect to the device over USB from a computer unless the owner has approved that step in writing.
4. Record what is seen, not what is expected. Leave "unknown" where unknown.
5. Any finding that suggests battery swelling, heat, leakage or instability stops the audit: do not charge the device; keep it away from family spaces and seek a battery-safety decision.
